Project Overview

Project Name: Real Estate Property Management System

Team: Java Spring Boot & Angular Full Stack Development Team

Purpose: Understanding Client Requirements and Presenting the Solution

Introduction

A comprehensive web-based solution for real estate businesses:

  • Register and manage agencies
  • Add and assign properties to vendors
  • Monitor all activities via an admin dashboard

Case Study 1: Client Onboarding & Agency Registration

Business Scenario: Register an agency and manage operations.

Challenges:

  • Manual paperwork and approvals
  • No centralized tracking

Solution:

  • Sign-up and verification
  • Agency registration with secure storage
  • Admin approval workflow

Outcome: Paperless onboarding with quality control

Case Study 2: Property Registration & Assignment

Business Scenario: Add and manage properties

Challenges:

  • Multiple property management issues
  • No tracking of property status

Solution:

  • Add property with details
  • Assign vendors and update status

Outcome: Efficient property tracking and vendor accountability

Case Study 3: Vendor Onboarding & Property Sales

Business Scenario: Vendors manage property sales

Challenges:

  • No digital vendor assignment
  • No performance tracking

Solution:

  • Vendor login and property access
  • Sales tracking and commission calculation

Outcome: Real-time sales visibility and secured transactions

Case Study 4: Admin Dashboard & Reporting

Business Scenario: Monitor the system with full control

Challenges:

  • No real-time monitoring
  • Lack of consolidated data

Solution:

  • Admin dashboard with full visibility
  • Live property status & reporting

Outcome: Better decision-making and operational control

Case Study 5: Admin Map View for Property Status Tracking

Business Scenario: Visualize property status on a map

Challenges:

  • No regional trend analysis
  • Hard to identify location-wise performance

Solution:

  • Google Maps integration for live status visualization
  • Color-coded pins for Available, Sold, On-Hold

Outcome: Geo-based insight for marketing and investment

Key Benefits of the System

  • ✔ End-to-End Digital Transformation
  • ✔ Automated Property Management
  • ✔ Vendor & Buyer Transparency
  • ✔ Centralized Admin Control
  • ✔ Scalability & Security (Spring Boot & Angular)
